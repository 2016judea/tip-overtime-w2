Tip & Overtime W-2s

Privacy

Effective 2026-11-02. Tip & Overtime W-2s is made by Brick and Mortar AI LLC, Saint Paul, Minnesota.

What we read from your Clover

Read-only, and only to work out the figures you see: your business name and time zone; your employees' names and roles; their clock-in and clock-out times and the cash tips they declared at clock-out; and, for each payment, its time, tender type, tip, refunded tip, service charge, and the employee on it. We never read card numbers, never write to your register and never move money.

What we keep

Your Clover merchant id, business name and time zone; your access tokens, encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM); and the answers you type in (pay rates, exempt status, workweek start, occupation codes, tip sharing, other pay). We do not store your register rows: they are read from Clover each time you open the report, and the math runs in your browser. We keep a short log of when the app was opened and how many rows it read, to fix problems.

What we never do

We never sell or share your data or your employees' data. We never send anything to the IRS, the SSA, or anyone else. We never contact your employees.

Stopping

Uninstall the app from your Clover dashboard and Clover stops our access. To have your saved answers and tokens deleted, write to aidan@brickandmortar.dev from the email on your Clover account and we delete them within 7 days.

Site analytics

This site counts page views with Vercel Web Analytics, which sets no cookies. The report page uses one cookie, to keep you signed in for 12 hours.
