How this was worked out

Pay: BLS Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics, May 2025, national, all industries (wages include tips). Each Treasury Tipped Occupation Code is matched to its related SOC code from the IRS list; where there are several, the one with the most jobs is used. Tax: 2026 federal rates and the $16,100 standard deduction (Rev. Proc. 2025-32), single filer, no other income, before credits. "Per $1,000" is the tax on the last $1,000 of taxable income. "Most it can save" assumes $25,000 of the pay (or all of it, if less) is qualified tips: it is the ceiling, not a typical figure. Social Security and Medicare tax (7.65%) and state income tax are not reduced by this deduction.

The range across occupations: the most it can save a median earner runs from $1,564 to $5,528. A median bartender ($34,340) saves $120 per $1,000 and at most $1,941.
