Tip & Overtime W-2s

Terms of service

Effective 2026-11-02. Tip & Overtime W-2s is made by Brick and Mortar AI LLC, Saint Paul, Minnesota.

What it is

A calculator. From your Clover register and the answers you give, it works out three figures per employee for the 2026 Form W-2: Box 12 code TP (cash tips reported to you), Box 12 code TT (qualified overtime compensation), and Box 14b (the Treasury Tipped Occupation Code), and it shows every row and step behind them.

What it is not

It does not file anything and is not tax, payroll or legal advice. You, or your accountant or payroll provider, review the figures and decide what goes on each W-2. The figures are only as right as the register and your answers: Clover does not hold pay rates, exempt status, tip sharing done off the register, or bonuses paid outside it, so the app asks you for them and flags every figure that rests on an answer you have not confirmed. The rules it applies are cited on the page: the IRS 2026 General Instructions for Forms W-2 and W-3, IRS FS-2026-13, the IRS list of tipped occupations, and DOL Fact Sheets #15, #23 and #56A.

Paying and stopping
