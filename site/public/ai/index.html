For your staff

Paste payroll rows (employee, week, hours, rate, tips, role) and get each person's W-2 box 12 TP, TT and box 14b.

The three tools

tip_overtime_deduction — the tips and overtime deductions from W-2 boxes or pay stubs, every Schedule 1-A line, the Form 1040 line, the federal tax saved, and a worksheet link.
w2_tip_overtime_boxes — W-2 box 12 TP, TT and box 14b per employee from payroll rows, with each overtime week's regular rate.
tipped_occupation_code — whether a job is on the IRS list, its box 14b code, and what the deduction is worth at that job's median pay.
