What it reads

From your Clover, read-only: employees, clock-ins and clock-outs, cash tips declared at clock-out, and every tip and service charge on a payment.

What it asks you

What Clover doesn't know: pay rates, who is exempt, your workweek's start day, tip sharing done off the register, bonuses, and each person's occupation code. Each one has a default you can see.

What it never does

File. We calculate and show the math. You or your accountant put it on the W-2s. Nothing is ever sent to the IRS or SSA.
